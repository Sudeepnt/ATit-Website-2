How we underwrite

Title first. Then everything else.

Eleven gates. An opportunity passes one before it reaches the next. Most never reach a capital partner. That is the point.

The sequence

Why legal comes before market.

In India, the biggest losses come from documents, not demand. So we read the documents first. Then the people who will build it price it.

The title checklist →

  1. Origination

    From mandates, landowners, banks, resolution professionals, ARCs, operators and partners.

  2. Screening

    Location, size, title status, complexity, partner fit.

  3. Title and legal

    Thirty-year title chain. Encumbrances. Litigation. Lender claims. External counsel.

  4. Technical and site

    Survey, access, soil, drainage, utilities, structure.

  5. Locality and market

    Demand and pricing, street by street. Infrastructure counted only when funded.

  6. Execution

    Our builders price the build, the approvals and the schedule.

  7. Structure and tax

    Ownership, stamp duty, GST, income tax, exit.

  8. The memo

    Thesis, risks in plain words, downside, plan, our role and fees.

  9. Your decision

    With your own advisers. Every document shared. Site visits arranged.

  10. Delivery

    Our Operating Platform executes the plan.

  11. Reporting and exit

    Fixed reporting rhythm. Exit planned on day one.

The title checklist

What we read on every parcel.

Indian title is presumptive. The record shows who is presumed to own the land, not who conclusively does. So we read everything. These are the Karnataka records.

01

Auction Properties

Bank SARFAESI · NCLT / IBC
ValueBuy below value. Fix title and possession.
Delivered byAdvisory, ADL, Infrastructure

Banks sell property every week. Most of it does not sell. Not because of location. Because of paperwork.

14.7%Bank auctions that succeed.
BAANKNET, Jul 2024 – Jun 2025
~10%Price cut after each failed round.
Lender practice, as reported
739 daysAverage insolvency resolution. The limit is 330.
IBBI, Dec 2025

Why it exists

Assets sell "as is, where is". Possession is often on paper only. Dues pass to the buyer. Borrowers challenge the sale. Most buyers walk away. Prices fall, round after round.

What we do

  • Read the title, the litigation and the dues before we bid.
  • Walk the site. Check who is in possession.
  • Bid against our own cost to fix it, not the reserve price.
  • Register, take possession, reposition. Our teams, start to finish.

What can go wrong

A sale set aside. Possession delayed. Hidden dues. We price each one before we recommend.

02

Greenfield Land Banking

Ahead of infrastructure
ValueInfrastructure arrives. Land re-prices.
Delivered byADL, Advisory, Development

In India, land value follows infrastructure. Roads, metros and airports re-price the land they reach. We own clean land before they arrive.

₹12.2 lakh crUnion capex target, FY27.
Union Budget 2026-27, as reported
3,772 acresClosed by developers in 2025. Up about 50%.
Anarock, Jan 2026
~600 mnUrban Indians by 2036.
Primus Partners, 2025

Why it exists

Land near future infrastructure is split across many small owners. Records are uneven. Most of it is not converted. Assembling it is slow and local. Institutions stay away.

What we do

  • Count infrastructure only when it is funded and under construction.
  • Verify every parcel. Assemble them into usable land.
  • Convert, zone and plan access before demand arrives.
  • Plan the exit on day one: sale, joint development, or our own build.

What can go wrong

Infrastructure arrives late. Late projects average 35 months behind. Greenfield is for partners who can wait.

03

Brownfield Redevelopment

Redevelop · Reposition · Reuse
ValueA better use for well-located land.
Delivered byDevelopment, Infrastructure, ADL

Great locations. Tired buildings. Old offices, single-screen cinemas, idle industrial plots. The location cannot be replaced. The use can.

Why it exists

Many owners are families without the capital or capability to reposition. Older buildings lose tenants to newer ones. The land is often worth more than the business on it.

What we do

  • Test what the land allows: FAR, TDR, demand.
  • Choose: renovate, reuse or rebuild.
  • Let owners keep their land through joint development or development management.
  • Re-lease it through our own team.

What can go wrong

Approvals. Structural surprises. Cost overruns. Our builders price them first.

04

Real Asset Ventures

The gravity thesis
ValueOperating income plus the value of the asset beneath it.
Delivered byAdvisory, Development, ADL, Infrastructure

Startups and enterprises whose business is anchored in a physical asset. Not only land and buildings: any venture whose advantage rests on a real asset it owns, leases or operates.

What we look for

  • The asset is the advantage

    The business is difficult to replicate without its site, its access or its permissions.

  • Two sources of value

    Operating income from the business, and the long-term value of the asset beneath it.

  • A role for our operating platform

    We can find, build, lease or expand the sites the business depends on.

What we do

  • Underwrite the asset and the business. Separately, then together.
  • Hold the asset and the operator side by side, or separate them through sale-and-leaseback.
  • Find, build and expand sites for the operator.

What can go wrong

The operator underperforms. The physical asset protects the downside. It does not remove business risk.

05

Distressed or Litigated

Complexity premium
ValueResolving claims that trap good land.
Delivered byAdvisory, ADL, Development, Infrastructure

Good land, trapped. In a failed joint development. Under a lender's claim. In a family dispute. In a stalled project with buyers waiting. Resolving the trap is the investment.

~66%Civil disputes over land or property.
DAKSH, 2015–16
22%Insolvency cases in real estate. About 1,900.
IBBI, Dec 2025
5.08 lakhStalled homes across 42 cities.
PropEquity, Aug 2024

Why it exists

Indian title is presumptive. Disputes are common and slow. Lenders, landowners, developers and buyers can all claim the same asset. Few can map every claim, settle them, then finish the building. Those who can are paid for it.

What we do

  • Map every party, every claim, every court. In one document.
  • Negotiate a resolution all parties can sign.
  • Bring in new capital and a capable builder where needed.
  • Finish the project with our own teams.

What can go wrong

Courts are slow. Rulings go the wrong way. We size each position to the realistic legal path, not the hoped-for one.

The problem

Projects rarely fail on design. They fail between design and site.

Too many hands. No single owner. Four leaks follow.

Coordination

Architects, consultants, contractors, suppliers. Different clocks. No one in charge.

Cost

Quantities never measured. Scope drifts. Prices move.

Time

Materials late. Labour missing. Weather unplanned.

Quality

Checked sometimes. Not built in.

Why we started

Capital was never the constraint.

One of us grew up in a real estate family office. Land, warehouses, hotels. The other grew up in a chartered accountancy practice. Audits, structures, tax.

We kept seeing the same thing. Good capital. Good land. Stuck. Weak title. Unmanaged contractors. Approvals nobody tracked. Owners who could not see their own land.

The problem was the distance between a capital decision and a delivered asset. So we built the operating platform first. Then the capital platform on top of it.

Work began in 2024. The firm was founded in 2025.

The loop

Operate. Learn. Originate. Deliver. Repeat.

Our capital partners get the capital platform and the operating platform. That is the loop we are closing.

  1. Operate

    Our teams work on real sites, for real owners.

  2. Learn

    Which titles hold. Which areas move. Which contractors deliver. What things really cost.

  3. Originate

    Owners, lenders and operators bring us deals first.

  4. Underwrite

    With costs we have paid. Structured so each partner's position is clear.

  5. Deliver and report

    The team that planned it builds it. Partners see everything.

  6. Compound trust

    Delivery earns the next mandate. The loop turns again.

How we work

Our values.

Our values guide every recommendation we make, especially when an opportunity looks attractive and the documents do not.

  • Title first

    We will not show you an opportunity until we have read its title. A good story on bad title is a bad asset.

  • We build what we underwrite

    If we cannot build it, lease it and run it, we will not recommend it.

  • You decide. We steward.

    You hold your position directly. You make every decision. We originate, diligence, deliver and report.

  • Our interests follow yours

    Where we recommend, we look to participate alongside. Our economics should follow the asset, not the activity.

  • Bad news travels first

    Delays, overruns and legal surprises reach you when we learn of them. With a plan.

  • Long horizon. Local depth.

    We would rather know one corridor completely than ten corridors a little. Land rewards patience.

  • Institutional from day one

    Documents, governance and reporting an institution would accept. Even when you are a family.

Stewardship

Responsible by design.

We act as a long-term owner would. That shapes how we choose assets, how we build and how we report.

Clean title, clear governance

Every asset begins with verified title and a documented ownership structure, so the next generation inherits assets rather than disputes.

Reuse before rebuild

Where a well-located building can be renovated or repositioned, we consider that first. The most efficient building is often the one already standing.

Safe, accountable sites

Safety incidents are reported to owners immediately. Contractors, labour and quality are managed by one accountable team.

Places people value

Through YaavArea, we intend to listen to residents and businesses, and let their experience inform where and how we build.

Transparent reporting

A long horizon
