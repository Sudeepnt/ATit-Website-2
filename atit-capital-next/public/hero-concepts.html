Ten versions of the homepage city

All ten are the same story: in a city, a plot is chosen, its title is checked, a crane builds on it, it fills with tenants, and it is looked after. Versions 1 to 5 keep the homepage look and change the building. Versions 6 to 10 give the same story a completely different look and feel. Tell us which one you prefer.

1 The Tower 2 The Stepped Tower 3 The Pair 4 The White Tower 5 The Living Block 6 The Full City 7 Street Level 8 The Architect's Model 9 The Sketchbook 10 The Skyline
1

The Tower

What's different: nothing. This is the version on the homepage now.

What it says to a visitor

One plot is picked out of the city and checked layer by layer. A single dark tower goes up on it, the floors light up, and the site is planted and looked after.

What they walk away with

One asset, chosen carefully and done properly.

2

The Stepped Tower

What's different: the tower narrows in three stages, with a thin gold line at each step and trees on the ledges.

What it says to a visitor

The same story, with a building that has a wide, solid base and gets lighter as it climbs. It feels more like a landmark, and the planted ledges show care after the build.

What they walk away with

Solid foundations, and something crafted on top of them.

3

The Pair

What's different: two slimmer towers of different heights rise from one shared base.

What it says to a visitor

The same story, but the crane works across two towers at once. It reads less like a single project and more like a platform that holds more than one asset.

What they walk away with

One careful process, more than one asset.

4

The White Tower

What's different: same shape as the homepage tower, but white with fine dark floor lines instead of dark teal.

What it says to a visitor

The same story, in a quieter key. The building belongs to the white city around it, so the gold windows become the strongest thing on screen once tenants arrive.

What they walk away with

Calm, understated, and earning quietly.

5

The Living Block

What's different: the homepage tower, but when it fills up, the windows across the city light up and more cars arrive.

What it says to a visitor

The same story, with a bigger ending. The light starts in the tower and spreads outward through the neighbouring buildings, so the asset is shown lifting everything around it.

What they walk away with

What they build makes the whole neighbourhood better.

Same story, a different look

Still a city, still land being taken up and built on. Each of these five has its own style.

6

The Full City

The look: the city fills the whole frame, edge to edge, with traffic on every road.

What it says to a visitor

A dense, busy city surrounds one empty plot. It gets marked, checked and built on, and the dark tower that rises becomes the one thing your eye goes to.

What they walk away with

In a crowded market, they find the one place worth building on.

7

Street Level

The look: real perspective and long shadows. The camera starts high and comes down to street level.

What it says to a visitor

We look down on the city and pick out the plot. As the crane works, the camera comes down to eye level, so the finished building stands in front of you against the sky.

What they walk away with

Something real, being built in a real city.

8

The Architect's Model

The look: a white scale model of the city on a round dark teal base.

What it says to a visitor

It feels like standing around the table in a planning room. The city is plain white card, and the only thing with real colour is the building being added to it.

What they walk away with

They plan every detail before anything happens on the ground.

9

The Sketchbook

The look: the city as a hand-drawn ink sketch on white paper. Only the new building gets colour.

What it says to a visitor

The whole city is pen and ink. The chosen plot and the building rising on it are painted in, teal and then gold, so your eye goes straight to the one thing being made real.

What they walk away with

A considered, personal way of working, far from a generic developer.

10

The Skyline

The look: a flat, side-on poster of the skyline in layers of pale blue-grey, with a gold sun climbing behind it.

What it says to a visitor

The city is seen from the side, with gaps in its skyline. The ground under one gap is examined, then a tower fills it and rises above its neighbours as the sun comes up.

What they walk away with

They change the skyline, and they start from the ground.

Which one do you prefer?

Send back the number. If you like a detail from one and the building from another, say so and we can combine them.
