How it runs

Eight stages. Drawings to handover.

We start building only when the design data is complete enough to price.

  1. Design intake

    Drawings, specs, schedules. Gaps listed before pricing.

  2. Estimate

    Quantities measured. Current rates. Budget agreed.

  3. Procurement plan

    What we buy, what contractors supply, and when.

  4. Award

    Like-for-like tenders. Your approval.

  5. Mobilise

    Site set-up, crews, safety induction, first deliveries.

  6. Build

    Daily supervision. Weekly reports. Inspections at every hold point.

  7. Measure and bill

    No payment without measurement and reconciliation.

  8. Hand over

    Snagging, as-builts, warranties. Then asset management.
