Ways to work with us

Five ways. One rule: you own it directly.

Every capital partner holds its position in its own name or its own structure. Every engagement has its own written agreement. We never pool money.

  • Dedicated mandates

    You engage us to find, buy and build an asset you own outright.

  • Direct co-investment with operators

    You invest in your own name in a real asset venture we have found and diligenced.

  • Joint development

    You own land, or want to. We structure the deal with the landowner and manage delivery on your side.

  • Portfolio stewardship

    You already own property. We audit it, fix it, manage it, or sell it.

  • Transaction advisory

    You are buying, selling or leasing. We diligence, price, negotiate and close.

ATit Capital is not registered with SEBI. It does not run a fund or a collective investment scheme. See disclosures.
