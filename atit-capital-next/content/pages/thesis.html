Focus Areas

We focus on select real asset categories in India where title, execution and capital meet, and where our operating platform gives us a distinct advantage.

Auction Properties

Bank and NCLT assets that fail on title, possession and process, acquired with the expertise to resolve them.

Greenfield Land Banking

Clean, assembled land on funded infrastructure corridors, secured before the corridor re-prices it.

Brownfield Redevelopment

Well-located land held back by an ageing building, repositioned to its highest and best use.

Real Asset Ventures

Startups and enterprises whose business is anchored in a physical asset, backed together with the asset itself.

Distressed & Litigated

Sound assets trapped by disputes, lender claims or stalled projects, unlocked through structured resolution.
