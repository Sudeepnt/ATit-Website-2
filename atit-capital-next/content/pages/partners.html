Working with us

Six steps. You decide at every one.

Nothing moves without a written agreement.

  1. Introduction

    A conversation with a founding partner.

  2. Discovery

    Your goals, horizon, constraints and current holdings.

  3. Engagement

    Scope, role, fees, reporting and conflicts. In writing.

  4. Opportunity or mandate

    A full memo, or the work you engaged us for.

  5. Delivery

    Our teams execute. Every material decision comes to you.

  6. Reporting

    A fixed rhythm, through to exit or long-term hold.
