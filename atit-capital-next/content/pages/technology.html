YaavArea Intelligence

Land is valued by the area around it. YaavArea Intelligence is being built to read that area, through the people who live and work in it, before capital is committed.

Does the road flood? Is the metro station actually being built? Do the schools work? No title deed records this. The people who live there know.

Residents

Rate the area

Services, amenities, institutions. A living record of how a place feels.

Businesses

Rated where they serve

Good operators stand out. Local demand becomes visible.

Civic bodies

Rated alongside

A public measure of service, area by area.

Why we are building it

Better signals. Better decisions.

  • Area signals in every opportunity memo.
  • Rising demand spotted before prices move.
  • Local evidence for the operators we back.
  • Quality-of-life evidence for GCC site selection.
  • Tenant and community feedback on the assets we steward.
YaavArea Intelligence is in development. What is described here is intent, not a live product. We will share progress as it ships.
